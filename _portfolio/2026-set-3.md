Let $n \ge 7$ be a positive integer. Weronika has an $n \times n$ field of unit squares in which to graze her $n$ elephants. To stop the elephants escaping, the border of the field is fenced off.

Weronika has to place each elephant in a unit square inside the field such that there is at most one elephant in each row and at most one elephant in each column. She also wants to put additional fences inside the field. A single fence covers the edge between two unit squares. She wants to add these fences so that the elephants are separated and unable to meet (a elephant can move between two unit squares if they share an edge that has not been covered by a fence, and two elephants can meet if they can move to two unit squares sharing an edge that has not been covered by a fence).

What is the minimum number of fences Weronika has to **add** so that both conditions can hold (the initial placement of the $n$ elephants having at most one elephant per row and column, and them not being able to meet afterwards) as a function of $n$? 


*Remark*: Weronika places the elephants **and** the fences, and wants the minimum given the ability to choose both.
