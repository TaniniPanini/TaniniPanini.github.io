Let $k$ be a circle centered at $O$ 
 and let $X, A, Y$ be three points on $k$ in this order such that the tangent to the circumcircle of triangle $OXA$ through $X$
 and the tangent to the circumcircle of $OAY$
 through $Y$ are parallel. Show that $XAY = 120^\circ$ if $A$ lies on the minor arc $XY$.

Remark: The minor arc $XY$ is the shorter arc of the circle $k$ linking $X$ and $Y$.
