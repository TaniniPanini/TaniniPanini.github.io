Teaching is my passion and over the years I have curated a large portfolio of teaching material and handouts. In particular, I love teaching mathematics and specifically Olympiad combinatorics. 

I have taught Olympiad students at meetings and camps at Lausanne, Zürich, Bern, Aarburg, [Les Diablerets](https://swissmaprs.ch/events/international-egmo-camp/), Bled (Slovenia), Thuwal (Saudi Arabia), Tokyo (Japan) and Brisbane (Australia), as well as online. 

I have been a teaching assistant for many university courses, and my favourite is Analysis I. You can see a full list on my [resume](/resume.html).
